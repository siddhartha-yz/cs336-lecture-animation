The square's edge is the equivalent probability per token. Flip it: this is like choosing one tile out of four equally likely ones. That equivalent number is perplexity. Four boxes measure difficulty. The actual vocabulary can be enormous.

[02:04.37–02:40.33 | 1-04]

Stack a bit here. Three more there. Spread the stack equally across the two positions. Raise two to that average and you're back on the perplexity scale. A single nearly impossible token makes a very tall stack.
